# Giraffes

## Introduction

Giraffes (genus Giraffa) are large African hoofed mammals. They are the tallest living terrestrial animals and the largest ruminants on Earth. They are classified under the family Giraffidae, along with their closest extant relative, the okapi.

## Giraffe Facts

1. The distinguishing characteristics of giraffes are their extremely long neck and legs, horn-like ossicones, and spotted coat patterns.

2. Their scattered range extends from Chad in the north to South Africa in the south and from Niger in the west to Somalia in the east.

3. Male giraffes use their necks as weapons in combat, a behaviour known as "necking".
